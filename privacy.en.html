Nydrax

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. In one sentence

NYDRAX doesn't collect any data. There's no account, there's no server, there's no network.

2. Where your data stays

The mixes you save, your favorite sounds and the timer settings stay in the app's memory, on your iPhone. They don't leave the device and nobody can see them, not even us.

3. No analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, it doesn't identify you and doesn't profile you. There's nothing to turn off because there's nothing collecting data.

4. Background audio and the lock screen

While the mix is playing, iOS shows the sound name and the artwork on the lock screen and in Control Centre: that information is drawn on the phone, in a system panel. It isn't sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it.

5. The purchase

The purchase goes through the App Store: the payment and account data are handled by Apple under its own policy. NYDRAX only receives confirmation that the purchase exists.

6. Data controller

Simone D'Agostino, Calle Ollerías 20, 29012 Málaga, Spain — dagostino.simone@icloud.com. Nydrax is built by one person, not by a company: there is no data protection officer other than the controller, because there is no processing to oversee.

7. Your rights

The GDPR gives you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Here there is nothing to ask us for: the data the app keeps is already on your iPhone, and deleting it is immediate and permanent. If you believe the processing is not compliant, you can contact the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Children and changes

Nydrax is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them: since nothing is collected, the question doesn't arise. If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated; if one day a server were needed, it would be a different app, with a different policy, and we would say so before publishing it.
