NYDRAX doesn't collect any data. There's no account, there's no server, there's no network.

Where your data stays

The mixes you save, your favorite sounds and the timer settings stay in the app's memory, on your iPhone. They don't leave the device and nobody can see them, not even us.

No analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, it doesn't identify you and doesn't profile you. There's nothing to turn off because there's nothing collecting data.

The purchase

The purchase goes through the App Store: the payment and account data are handled by Apple under its own policy. NYDRAX only receives confirmation that the purchase exists.

NYDRAX plays sounds and is not a medical device: it doesn't cure insomnia and doesn't give clinical advice.
