Notice

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. In one sentence

NYDRAX plays sounds to accompany sleep. It is not a medical device and has no diagnostic or therapeutic purpose.

2. What it does

It plays fifteen recorded or generated sounds, in a continuous loop, with the option to layer up to three of them and adjust their volume. The sound turns off by itself according to the timer you choose.

3. What it doesn't do

It does not cure insomnia, does not assess sleep quality, does not measure anything and does not give clinical advice. If sleep is a health problem, the right person to see is a doctor.

4. Volume

Listen at a volume that lets you hear the sounds around you. A high volume for many hours can strain your hearing.

5. If you are unwell right now

Insomnia that lasts for weeks, anxiety and sleep apnoea are health conditions and they can be treated: if you wake up gasping or you no longer sleep, talk to a doctor. In an emergency call the emergency services of your country.
