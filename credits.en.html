Twelve sounds come from Pixabay, under the Pixabay Content License: commercial use allowed and no attribution required. This page is a courtesy.

2. The three noises

White, pink and brown noise are generated with ffmpeg (the `anoisesrc` noise source), they don't come from a recording, and they are free of rights. Their loop is perfect: exactly sixty seconds, with no seam.

3. What the license allows

The sounds are used inside the app as part of the listening experience. The license does not allow redistributing them as a separate library, and NYDRAX doesn't do that.
